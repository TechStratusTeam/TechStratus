Technology Strategy for Small Organizations

Good technology decisions are about more than buying new software or replacing old computers.

They involve understanding how your people work, where your systems create friction, what risks exist, and which investments will actually support your goals.

TechStratus helps organizations evaluate technology from a broader perspective and turn that evaluation into clear priorities.

This may include

  • Reviewing your current technology environment
  • Identifying gaps and unnecessary complexity
  • Prioritizing improvements
  • Planning future investments
  • Evaluating vendors and platforms
  • Building a practical technology roadmap

How Technology Consulting Differs From Managed IT

Technology Consulting and Business IT Services work closely together, but they serve different purposes.

Technology Consulting focuses on

  • Assessing
  • Planning
  • Evaluating
  • Prioritizing
  • Recommending

Business IT Services focuses on

  • Implementing
  • Supporting
  • Monitoring
  • Maintaining
  • Troubleshooting

A consulting engagement may determine that your organization should migrate to a new cloud platform. Business IT Services can then help implement and support that environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Technology Consulting the same as managed IT?

No. Managed IT focuses on day-to-day support and maintenance. Technology Consulting focuses on planning, strategy, decision-making, and long-term direction.

Do I need a large business to benefit from technology consulting?

No. Small organizations often benefit the most because they usually do not have a dedicated technology leader on staff.

Can you act like a fractional CIO?

Yes. For organizations that need ongoing strategic guidance, TechStratus can provide fractional technology leadership similar to a fractional CIO or technology advisor.

Can you help us decide between different software platforms?

Yes. TechStratus can help compare platforms based on cost, features, complexity, workflow, and long-term fit.

Can you help us create a technology budget?

Yes. Budget planning can include hardware replacement, software subscriptions, security improvements, cloud services, and future technology projects.

Do you sell the products you recommend?

Recommendations are intended to be based on what best fits the organization. If TechStratus also provides implementation or ongoing support, that relationship will be clearly explained.

Can you help manage a technology project?

Yes. TechStratus can assist with planning, vendor coordination, documentation, implementation oversight, and project organization for appropriately sized projects.
