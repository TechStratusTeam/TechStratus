Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer on-demand or ongoing IT support?

Both. You can reach out when something needs attention, or arrange ongoing help if you'd rather have someone looking after your technology. Tell us what your business needs and we'll suggest what fits.

What types of organizations do you support?

TechStratus primarily supports small businesses, nonprofits, churches, professional offices, and other local organizations.

Do you come to our office?

On-site help is available in the Missoula area, and many problems can also be fixed remotely.

Do I need a contract?

Not for one-time help. Ongoing service agreements are available if you'd like regular support.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

Just ask. Tell us what's going on and we'll suggest a practical next step.
