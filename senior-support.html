Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help my parents even if I live somewhere else?

Yes. TechStratus can work directly with family members to coordinate support while respecting the privacy and preferences of everyone involved.

Can you help protect seniors from scams?

Yes. TechStratus can help identify warning signs, strengthen security settings, and develop safer technology habits.

Do you provide in-home support?

Yes. Both remote and in-home support options are available depending on location and scheduling.

What devices do you support?

TechStratus supports many common computers, tablets, smartphones, printers, routers, streaming devices, and smart-home products.

What should someone do if they believe they have been targeted by a scam?

The first step is to remain calm. Depending on the situation, it may be necessary to change passwords, contact financial institutions, review accounts, preserve information, or notify the proper authorities.

TechStratus can help explain the next steps and improve the security of affected devices and accounts.
