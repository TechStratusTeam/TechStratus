A Complete IT Partner for Your Organization

Running a business involves more moving technology parts than most people realize. Computers, cloud platforms, networks, security tools, backups, and vendor relationships all have to work together, and when one piece breaks down, the effects are felt across the entire organization.

TechStratus provides complete IT support built around this reality. From day-to-day help desk assistance and device management to cloud migrations, network support, cybersecurity, and ongoing monitoring, every service is designed to work together as one dependable system rather than a patchwork of separate fixes.

The sections below outline each part of that support in more detail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is support available around the clock?

Monitoring systems operate continuously, but support availability depends upon the service agreement in place.

What does continuous monitoring mean?

Monitoring software continually checks the status of managed devices and generates alerts when problems are detected. Continuous monitoring should not be confused with continuous human supervision.

How quickly will you respond?

Response times vary according to the service plan selected and the nature of the issue.

What types of organizations do you support?

TechStratus primarily supports small businesses, nonprofits, churches, professional offices, and other local organizations.

Do you support servers?

Support capabilities depend upon the environment involved. Some server-related support is available, while highly specialized environments may require additional resources.

Is on-site support included?

On-site assistance is available and may be included depending upon the service agreement selected.

Do you require a contract?

Both project-based engagements and ongoing service agreements are available.
