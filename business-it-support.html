Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of problems can you help with?

Everyday business technology problems: computers and software, email and cloud accounts, Wi-Fi and network issues, and new device setup. If you're not sure, just ask.

Do you come to my office, or can you help remotely?

Both. Many problems can be fixed remotely, which is faster and more convenient. If it needs hands-on help, we can schedule a visit in the Missoula area.

Do I need a contract?

No. Business IT Support is on-demand, so you can reach out whenever you need help. If you'd like regular, ongoing help, ongoing service agreements are available.

How does pricing work?

We'll talk through the problem first, then explain what it involves and what it costs before any work begins. Pricing depends on the issue or project.

Can you provide ongoing IT support?

Many businesses start with on-demand help and add ongoing support as their needs grow. Tell us what you're looking for and we'll talk through the options that fit your business.

How quickly can you help?

Response times vary with the issue and our schedule. If something is urgent, calling is the fastest way to reach us.

What types of organizations do you support?

TechStratus primarily supports small businesses, nonprofits, churches, professional offices, and other local organizations.
