Frequently Asked Questions

What is fractional IT leadership?

It's ongoing, senior-level technology guidance without hiring a full-time IT director or manager. We help you decide what to invest in, keep projects moving, and make sure your technology supports your goals.

Who is this for?

Small businesses, nonprofits, churches, and professional offices that rely on technology but don't have someone in-house to guide it.

How is this different from Business IT Support?

Business IT Support fixes problems when they come up. Fractional IT Leadership is about the bigger picture: planning, decisions, budgets, and vendors.

Can you work with our existing IT provider?

Yes. We can coordinate with the providers and vendors you already use and help you decide whether they're the right fit.

Can you help with AI decisions?

Yes. We can help you decide where AI tools make sense for your organization and where they don't. For hands-on setup, see AI & Business Automation.

How does it work, and what does it cost?

We start with a conversation about your organization and what you need. Then we agree on a clear scope and arrangement, and explain the cost, before we begin.

Do you require a contract?

Ongoing guidance is set up as a service agreement. We'll explain the terms in plain language before you commit.
