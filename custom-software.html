What We Build

When off-the-shelf tools don't fit, we design and build software around your process.

Internal Business Tools

Dashboards, trackers, and admin tools that replace scattered spreadsheets and manual steps.

Customer & Client Portals

Secure logins, forms, and self-service pages that make it easier for people to work with you.

Integrations & Automations

Connect the systems you already use so information moves between them without retyping.

How It Works

  1. Step 1

    Discovery

    We learn how your team works today, what slows you down, and what the software needs to do.

  2. Step 2

    Design & Planning

    We outline the features and scope, then agree on a plan and a clear quote before building.

  3. Step 3

    Build & Review

    We build in stages, and you review working versions along the way so there are no surprises.

  4. Step 4

    Launch & Support

    We launch the software, help your team get started, and offer ongoing support if you want it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of software can you build?

Internal business tools, client or customer portals, and integrations that connect the systems you already use. If you are not sure your idea fits, tell us what you are trying to solve and we will talk it through.

When does custom software make sense?

When off-the-shelf tools do not fit your process, or your team is working around them with spreadsheets and manual steps. If an existing tool would do the job for less, we will tell you.

Do you build mobile or desktop apps?

TechStratus focuses on web-based tools, portals, and integrations. If you need a native mobile or desktop app, tell us about the project and we will let you know whether it is a good fit.

How does a project work, and what does it cost?

We start with a conversation about what you need, then provide a clear quote before building. Projects are delivered in stages so you can review progress along the way. Cost depends on the scope.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Ongoing support and maintenance are available if you want them, so your software keeps working as your business changes.

How is this different from AI & Business Automation?

AI & Business Automation focuses on setting up existing AI platforms and workflow tools. Custom Software Design is for software built specifically for your business.

Do you require a contract?

Both project-based engagements and ongoing service agreements are available.
