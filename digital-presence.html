Our Process

A successful digital project begins with understanding your organization before designing anything.

  1. Step 1

    Discovery

    We start by learning about your business, audience, goals, existing materials, and challenges.

  2. Step 2

    Strategy

    Together, we determine what needs to be created, improved, or changed and establish a clear direction for the project.

  3. Step 3

    Design & Development

    The website, brand, campaign, or other materials are created based on the agreed direction.

  4. Step 4

    You have an opportunity to review the work, provide feedback, and refine the final product.

  6. Step 6

    Maintain

    When needed, TechStratus can continue supporting your website, social media, or other digital platforms after launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to use TechStratus for my website, branding, and marketing?

No. Digital Presence services can be used individually or together. TechStratus can build a website, develop a brand, manage social media, or work with the materials and systems you already have. When multiple services are needed, they can be coordinated to create a more consistent presence across your business.

Can you redesign my existing website or brand?

Yes. You do not have to start from scratch. TechStratus can evaluate your existing website or visual identity and recommend what should be kept, improved, or replaced. Sometimes a thoughtful refresh is more practical than a complete redesign.

Can you maintain my website after it launches?

Yes. Ongoing website maintenance options are available for businesses that would rather not manage updates, content changes, troubleshooting, and routine maintenance themselves.

Do you offer ongoing social media management?

Yes. TechStratus can provide ongoing social media support, including content planning, content creation, account maintenance, campaigns, and basic performance reporting. Services can be tailored to the platforms and level of support your business actually needs.

Will you help with SEO?

Yes. Website projects can include foundational search engine optimization, such as page structure, titles, descriptions, content organization, mobile usability, and other basic on-page SEO practices. More extensive SEO campaigns can be discussed separately based on your goals.

Can you work with an existing logo and brand?

Absolutely. If you already have a strong visual identity, TechStratus can work within your existing brand rather than replacing it. The goal is consistency, not change for the sake of change.

How much does a website, branding project, or marketing service cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of the project, the amount of content or design work required, and whether you need one-time or ongoing support. TechStratus will establish the scope, expectations, and pricing before work begins.

How long does a digital project take?

Timelines vary depending on the project. A small branding project or landing page may move quickly, while a complete website or brand identity requires more planning, development, and review. A realistic timeline will be established before the project begins.
