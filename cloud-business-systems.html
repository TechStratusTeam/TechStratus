What's Included

The right cloud tools make it easier for your team to communicate, collaborate, and access what they need. TechStratus helps organize and manage the systems behind your daily work.

  • File sharing and collaboration setup
  • User access and permissions management

  • Password and account support
  • Cloud backup setup and management
  • Assistance choosing and configuring business tools
  • Ongoing administration and support

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "cloud management" mean?

It means keeping the systems behind your daily work organized: where files are stored, who can access them, how email works, and how new employees get set up. TechStratus handles that so your team can focus on their work.

Can you set up and manage our business email?

Yes. TechStratus can set up and administer business email and related accounts.

Can you help when employees join or leave?

Yes. Onboarding and offboarding support includes accounts, access, and permissions, so the right people have the right access.

Can you help us choose business tools?

Yes. TechStratus can help compare and configure tools based on cost, features, complexity, and long-term fit.

How quickly will you respond?

Response times vary according to the service plan selected and the nature of the issue.

What types of organizations do you support?

TechStratus primarily supports small businesses, nonprofits, churches, professional offices, and other local organizations.

Do you require a contract?

Both project-based engagements and ongoing service agreements are available.
