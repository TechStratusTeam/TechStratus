What's Included

Your brand is more than a logo. TechStratus helps businesses develop a consistent visual identity and message that reflects who they are and connects with the people they want to reach.

Services may include

  • Logo design and refinement
  • Brand color selection
  • Font and typography selection

  • Rebranding and brand refreshes

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work with an existing logo and brand?

Absolutely. If you already have a strong visual identity, TechStratus can work within your existing brand rather than replacing it. The goal is consistency, not change for the sake of change.

Can you refresh my existing brand?

Yes. You do not have to start from scratch. TechStratus can evaluate your existing visual identity and recommend what to keep, improve, or replace.

What will I receive from a branding project?

The deliverables are defined up front. For a larger project, that might include logo variations, a color palette, and a short brand guide.

Can you create graphics for my website and social media?

Yes. Branded graphics for websites and social media are part of our branding services.

How much does branding cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of the project, the amount of content or design work required, and whether you need one-time or ongoing support. TechStratus will establish the scope, expectations, and pricing before work begins.

How long does a branding project take?

Timelines vary depending on the project. A small branding project may move quickly, while a complete brand identity requires more planning, development, and review. A realistic timeline will be established before the project begins.
