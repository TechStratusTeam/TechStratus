Frequently Asked Questions

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. You do not have to start from scratch. TechStratus can evaluate your existing website and recommend what should be kept, improved, or replaced. Sometimes a thoughtful refresh is more practical than a complete redesign.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. TechStratus can build sites with online sales, customer accounts, and other interactive features, not just simple information pages.

Will my website work on phones and tablets?

Yes. TechStratus designs websites to work well on phones, tablets, and computers.

Will you help with SEO?

Yes. Website projects can include foundational search engine optimization, such as page structure, titles, descriptions, content organization, mobile usability, and other basic on-page SEO practices. More extensive SEO campaigns can be discussed separately based on your goals.

Can you maintain my website after it launches?

Yes. Ongoing website care is available, including updates, backups, security, and troubleshooting.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of the project, the amount of content or design work required, and whether you need one-time or ongoing support. TechStratus will establish the scope, expectations, and pricing before work begins.

How long does a website take?

Timelines vary depending on the project. A landing page may move quickly, while a complete website requires more planning, development, and review. A realistic timeline will be established before the project begins.
