About TechStratus

Technology has become essential to almost every part of modern life and business. It also seems to become more complicated every year.

TechStratus exists to make that technology easier to understand, safer to use, and more effective for the people who depend on it.

The TechStratus Story

Practical Technology Help for Real People

Technology companies often fall into one of two categories. Consumer support can feel impersonal and focused on fixing a single device, while business technology providers can be designed around organizations with large budgets, dedicated IT departments, and complex enterprise needs.

There is a lot of room in between.

TechStratus was created to serve the people and organizations who need knowledgeable technology support without all the unnecessary complexity.

For individuals and families, that means having someone who can patiently explain technology, solve problems, improve online safety, and provide trusted guidance.

For small businesses and organizations, it means having access to IT support, websites and digital presence, AI and automation, and technology strategy without needing multiple providers or a large internal team.

The approach is simple: understand the problem first, recommend what actually makes sense, and help make technology work better.

The TechStratus Approach

  • Listen First

    Technology recommendations should begin with understanding the problem.

    Before recommending a new device, software platform, subscription, or service, TechStratus starts by learning what you are trying to accomplish.

    Sometimes the best solution is new technology. Sometimes it is making better use of what you already have.

  • Explain Things Clearly

    Technology does not need to be buried beneath technical terminology.

    Whether explaining a problem to a home user or discussing a technology investment with a business owner, the goal is to communicate clearly enough that you understand your options and can make an informed decision.

  • Recommend What You Actually Need

    More technology is not always better technology.

    TechStratus focuses on solutions appropriate to your needs, budget, and level of complexity. Whenever possible, recommendations are based on what provides the best fit rather than what creates the largest sale.

  • Set Realistic Expectations

    Clear expectations build better relationships.

    TechStratus is a small technology company, not a 24-hour enterprise help desk. Services are designed around capabilities that can be delivered reliably and professionally.

    If something falls outside those capabilities, you deserve to know that.

  • Under-Promise and Over-Deliver

    It is better to establish realistic expectations and exceed them than make promises that cannot consistently be kept.

    That philosophy influences everything from response times and project schedules to managed IT services and ongoing support.

  • Build Relationships

    The best technology support gets better over time.

    When your technology provider understands your devices, systems, business, family, and goals, it becomes easier to solve problems and make better recommendations.

    TechStratus aims to become a trusted technology resource you can return to as your needs change.

Trust Matters

Technology providers are often given access to important devices, accounts, business systems, and personal information. That responsibility should be taken seriously.

  • Insured

    TechStratus maintains appropriate business insurance as part of operating professionally and responsibly.

  • Secure Tools

    Professional tools and security practices are used when accessing, monitoring, or supporting customer technology.

  • Respect for Confidentiality

    Information encountered while providing support is treated with care and discretion. Access is limited to what is reasonably necessary to perform the requested work.

  • Professional Standards

    Clients deserve clear communication, realistic expectations, responsible handling of technology, and transparency about the limits of a service.

  • Recommendations Without Unnecessary Sales Pressure

    Whenever practical, TechStratus aims to recommend technology based on what fits the customer's needs rather than a particular manufacturer or vendor.

    If a recommendation involves a product, service, partnership, referral arrangement, or other financial interest that could influence the recommendation, that relationship should be communicated clearly.
