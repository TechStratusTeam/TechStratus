Frequently Asked Questions

Do you help individuals, or businesses too?

Both. We help families and seniors with everyday technology, and we help small businesses with websites, branding, social media, cloud systems, and AI.

Do you come to my home, or can you help remotely?

Both. Many problems can be fixed remotely, which is faster and more convenient. If needed, we can also schedule an in-home visit.

What kinds of problems can you help with?

Everyday technology issues, like:

  • Slow computers
  • Wi-Fi and internet problems
  • Device setup
  • Password and email help
  • Online safety and scams

If you're not sure, just ask.

How do you use AI in your work?

AI helps us work efficiently, but every project is guided by human judgment and careful review. AI is a tool, not a replacement for people.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Ongoing website care, cloud administration, and support are available, and many clients choose monthly help so they always have someone to call.

Is my information private and secure?

Absolutely. We treat your information with care, never share personal data, and follow secure practices.
