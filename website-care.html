Website Care & Maintenance

Keep your website secure, current, and working for your business.

Explore Website Care See What's Included

What's Included

A website needs attention after it launches. TechStratus helps keep your site available, up to date, and aligned with your business, whether we built it or you already have one.

Services may include

  • Website backups and recovery support
  • Security updates and improvements
  • Uptime monitoring
  • Website updates and routine maintenance
  • Event, promotion, and content changes
  • Search engine optimization improvements
  • Website performance monitoring
  • Monthly activity and performance reports
  • Troubleshooting and technical support

How It Works

  1. Step 1

    Review

    We look at how your site is built, hosted, and backed up, and what it needs.

  2. Step 2

    Protect

    Backups, security updates, and uptime monitoring are put in place.

  3. Step 3

    Maintain

    Routine updates, content changes, and performance checks keep it running smoothly.

  4. Step 4

    Report

    Monthly reports show how your site is doing, and help is a call away when something comes up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you take care of a website you didn't build?

Yes. Website care is available for sites TechStratus builds and for existing sites we are able to support.

What does uptime monitoring mean?

Monitoring software continually checks that your website is available and generates an alert when there is a problem. It should not be confused with continuous human supervision.

Will I get reports?

Yes. Monthly activity and performance reports show how your website is doing.

Can you make updates for events, promotions, or other changes?

Yes. Event, promotion, and content changes are part of website care.

How quickly will you respond to problems?

Response times vary according to the service plan selected and the nature of the issue.

Do you require a contract?

Both project-based engagements and ongoing service agreements are available.
