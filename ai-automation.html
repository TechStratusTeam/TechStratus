Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to understand AI before getting started?

No. TechStratus can start with the basics and help you understand what AI can realistically do for your organization.

Which AI platforms do you support?

Support may include commonly used business AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and Google Gemini, along with selected automation and productivity tools.

Can you build custom AI systems?

TechStratus primarily focuses on implementing and integrating existing AI platforms and practical automation tools for small organizations. More advanced custom software development, machine learning systems, or enterprise AI infrastructure may require a specialized development partner.

Is it safe to give business information to AI?

It depends on the platform, account configuration, type of information, and your organization's requirements. TechStratus can help establish basic guidelines for safer AI use and identify information that should not be entered into unapproved tools.

Will AI replace employees?

That is not the objective of TechStratus AI services. The focus is on helping people reduce repetitive work, access information more efficiently, and spend more time on tasks that require human expertise, judgment, creativity, and relationships.

Can you automate tasks between our existing software?

In many cases, yes. Whether a process can be automated depends on the applications involved, available integrations, and complexity of the workflow. TechStratus can evaluate the process before recommending a solution.

Can you train our employees?

Yes. Training can be tailored to your organization's tools, workflows, employee roles, and level of AI experience.
