Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide in-home support?

Yes. TechStratus offers both remote and in-person support, depending on the nature of the problem and your location.

Can you help people who are not comfortable with technology?

Absolutely. One of the primary goals of TechStratus is to make technology easier to understand and use with confidence.

Can you help protect seniors from scams?

Yes. TechStratus can help improve security settings, identify common warning signs, organize accounts, and create stronger safeguards against fraud and scams.

Can you help families create safer online environments for children and teenagers?

Yes. TechStratus can help configure parental controls, strengthen privacy settings, improve security, and guide families toward healthier technology habits.

What kinds of devices do you support?

TechStratus supports many common computers, phones, tablets, printers, routers, streaming devices, smart-home products, and other consumer technology.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Ongoing support options are available for people who would like a trusted source of assistance whenever they need it.
