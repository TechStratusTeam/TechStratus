Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help us set up parental controls?

Yes. TechStratus can help configure controls for computers, phones, tablets, streaming devices, gaming systems, and wireless networks.

Can you help us create healthier technology habits?

Yes. Technology tools are important, but healthy habits, clear expectations, and open communication are equally important.

Can you help protect children and teenagers online?

Yes. TechStratus can help strengthen privacy settings, establish safeguards, improve account security, and identify areas of concern.

Will you monitor my child's online activity?

No. The goal is to help families build effective systems, stronger safeguards, and healthier technology practices. Parents remain responsible for decisions regarding monitoring and supervision.

Can you help us organize all of our devices and accounts?

Absolutely. TechStratus can help simplify passwords, subscriptions, cloud storage, backups, and household technology systems.
