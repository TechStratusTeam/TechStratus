Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help us set up parental controls?

Yes. TechStratus can help configure controls for computers, phones, tablets, streaming devices, gaming systems, and wireless networks.

Can you help us create healthier technology habits?

Yes. Technology tools are important, but healthy habits, clear expectations, and open communication are equally important.

Can you help protect children and teenagers online?

Yes. TechStratus can help strengthen privacy settings, establish safeguards, improve account security, and identify areas of concern.

Will you monitor my child's online activity?

TechStratus does not monitor your children for you. We can set up tools, including internet activity tracking and reporting on your home network, that help you see what is happening. Parents remain responsible for decisions regarding monitoring and supervision.

Can you help us organize all of our devices and accounts?

Absolutely. TechStratus can help simplify passwords, subscriptions, cloud storage, backups, and household technology systems.

Can you improve our home Wi-Fi?

Yes. TechStratus can identify coverage problems, optimize existing equipment, and install a router or mesh network when needed.

Can you set up our smart home and streaming devices?

Yes. We can install and set up smart home devices and entertainment systems, and connect them to your home network.

Can you convert old VHS tapes and DVDs to digital?

Yes. Family photo and video backups include VHS and DVD to digital conversion, so your memories are preserved and easy to access.

Do you provide in-home support?

Yes. TechStratus offers both remote and in-person support, depending on the nature of the problem and your location.
