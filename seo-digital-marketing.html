Frequently Asked Questions

What is SEO?

Search engine optimization (SEO) makes it easier for people searching on Google and other search engines to find your business. For a local business, that often means showing up when someone nearby searches for what you offer.

What is local SEO?

Local SEO helps your business appear in searches tied to a place, such as "accountant in Missoula," including Google Maps and your Google Business Profile.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and be cautious of anyone who does. Search results depend on many things outside anyone's control. We focus on practical improvements that help customers find you and trust what they find.

How long does it take to see results?

SEO is gradual. Some improvements, like updating your Google Business Profile, can help quickly, while others build over several months. We'll set realistic expectations up front.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. We can work with your existing site and recommend what to improve. If a new website would help, we'll explain why. See Website Design & Development.

Can you manage Google Ads and other paid campaigns?

Yes. We can set up and manage paid campaigns, and report on how they're performing in plain language. Your ad budget is set by you and is separate from our fees.

Do you help with email marketing?

Yes. We can help plan, design, and send email campaigns, and build landing pages that turn interest into inquiries.

Is this a one-time service or ongoing?

Both are available. You can start with a one-time SEO review or Google Business Profile setup, or choose an ongoing monthly plan for SEO or campaign management with clear deliverables.

Can you help with social media too?

Yes. See Social Media Management. We can coordinate your website, search presence, and social channels so they work together.
